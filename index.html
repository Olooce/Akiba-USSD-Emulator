How to use

  • Dial *144#, then press Call or the left soft key.
  • Type a menu number and press Send (left soft key, Call or OK).
  • Reply 0 to go back. The right soft key clears a digit, or cancels when the reply is empty.
  • End closes the session.
  • Keyboard: digits, * #, Enter = Call, Esc = End, Backspace = Clear.

Jinsi ya kutumia

  • Piga *144#, kisha bonyeza Piga au kitufe cha kushoto.
  • Andika nambari ya menyu na ubonyeze Tuma (kitufe cha kushoto, Piga au OK).
  • Jibu 0 kurudi nyuma. Kitufe cha kulia hufuta tarakimu, au hughairi jibu likiwa tupu.
  • Maliza hufunga huduma.
  • Kibodi: tarakimu, * #, Enter = Piga, Esc = Maliza, Backspace = Futa.
